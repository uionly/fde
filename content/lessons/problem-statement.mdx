## Requests are evidence, not specifications

“We need a chatbot” tells you something about the stakeholder's proposed solution. It tells you almost nothing about the broken workflow, affected user, or cost of the status quo.

<FDEPrinciple variant="insight">
  Translate a requested feature into an affected user, a failing workflow, a baseline, and a target outcome.
</FDEPrinciple>

## A useful problem statement

A strong statement names **who** experiences **what problem**, in **which workflow**, causing **what measurable impact**, under **which constraints**.

<CustomerScenario>
  Northstar support specialists spend 31% of handling time searching across three policy repositories. The target is to reduce median resolution time without exposing manager-only procedures.
</CustomerScenario>
