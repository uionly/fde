## The work starts before the specification

A Software Engineer is often given a bounded requirement. A Forward Deployed Engineer is more likely to hear a symptom, an executive mandate, or a loosely formed idea—and must discover what is worth building.

<CustomerScenario title="Northstar's opening request">
  Northstar Financial says: **“Build an AI customer-service agent.”** You do not yet know which workflow is slow, who may access which data, or how success will be measured.
</CustomerScenario>

<FDEPrinciple>
  Validate the problem before optimizing the solution.
</FDEPrinciple>

## End-to-end ownership

The FDE stays accountable across discovery, architecture, implementation, evaluation, deployment, adoption, and measurable impact. Shipping is an important milestone; customer value is the finish line.

<Callout title="Your first move">
  Map the current support workflow and establish a baseline before selecting a model or agent architecture.
</Callout>
